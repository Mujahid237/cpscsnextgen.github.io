Message of Chief Patron

Inspired by the epoch-making ideal “The sky is the limit”, Cantonment Public School and College, Saidpur (CPSCS) has grown into a beacon of enlightenment, aspiration, and academic excellence. Since its founding, the institution has been dedicated to shaping young minds through holistic and transformative education.

At CPSCS, we believe true education goes beyond textbooks. We aim to instill intellectual excellence, moral integrity, and practical life skills—empowering students to meet modern challenges with confidence, resilience, and creativity. Our focus on science, technology, and ethical reasoning forms the foundation for lifelong growth and success. In today’s digital era, strong technological infrastructure is essential. As Chief Patron of CPSCS, I am proud to launch our interactive website and digital management system—a platform designed to enhance transparency, engagement, and learning for students, parents, and educators.

This digital initiative serves as a vital link between guardians and the school’s academic and administrative bodies, fostering collaboration, improving communication, and supporting continuous progress.

I offer my heartfelt best wishes for the success of this endeavor. May this platform grow and enrich all it touches, and may everyone at CPSCS be blessed with wisdom, innovation, and success.

We heartily welcome your profound insights as we move forward together.

Warm Regards,

Maj Gen A B M Faisal Baten, ndc, psc

Chairman's Message

Education is more than acquiring information, the spirit of “Knowledge is power”, it awakens consciousness, dispels ignorance, and unlocks human potentials. It nurtures purpose, identity, and pride. Patriotism flourishes when grounded in a clear understanding of our history, culture, and literature—the pillars of our national heritage. In this fast-changing digital era, embracing educational innovation is essential. At Cantonment Public School and College, Saidpur (CPSCS), we are committed to nurturing adaptable, lifelong learners who can lead our nation toward a brighter future.

We believe that student success depends on overall well-being. Apart from academic mentoring process, CPSCS ensures physical and mental health support through an on-campus medical officer, medical assistant, and a dedicated student counselor for emotional guidance and motivation. To support students from distant areas, we provide secure hostel facilities for both boys and girls. Furthermore, school transportation is available from Parbatipur and Nilphamari to ensure a smooth commute.

I warmly invite our respected guardians, dear students, and valued visitors to regularly visit our official website for updates on CPSCS activities and achievements. Your continued engagement is vital to our shared success.

Let us unite in shaping a future rooted in excellence, integrity, and progress.

Warm Regards,

Brig Gen Muhammad Masudur Rahman, psc

Commander 222 Infantry Brigade & Station Commander, Saidpur Cantonment.

Principal's Message

Education lays the bedrock of national progress—it nurtures minds, empowers individuals, and inspires generations. Guided by our motto, “The sky is the limit,” Cantonment Public School and College, Saidpur (CPSCS) began its journey on April 4, 1979, with a vision to shape skilled, enlightened, and value-driven citizens. Over the years, CPSCS has emerged as a symbol of academic excellence both nationally and internationally. The institution has received the Best Educational Institution Award multiple times and most recently ranked 4th among all Bangladesh Army-administered institutions. Our students have also earned global recognition by winning prestigious international awards.

In pursuit of quality education, CPSCS implements diverse strategies—multimedia classrooms, special assessments, tutorial sessions, and motivational programs. A prefect system promotes student leadership, while various co-curricular clubs foster individual talents. To personalize instruction, we use a classification model identifying students as: Willing and Able, Willing but Unable, Unwilling but Able, and Unwilling and Unable.

This approach allows our experienced faculty to provide targeted support, ensuring every learner receives the guidance they need. CPSCS also prioritizes teacher development through regular training programs to enhance instructional quality. Our digital automation system and interactive website offer easy access to results, attendance, fees, and reports—promoting transparency and timely communication. Creativity remains central to our approach, with creative learning boards encouraging critical thinking and innovation. Our alumni continue to excel at home and abroad, while many current students have secured admission to medical, engineering, and top public universities nationwide.

As Principal, I am proud to lead a committed team dedicated to educational excellence. CPSCS stands firm in its mission to develop future leaders who are academically strong, socially responsible, and globally aware.

I welcome our guardians, students, and well-wishers to share your ideas and join us in shaping a brighter future. May Allah bless us in all endeavors.

Warm Regards,

Principal
