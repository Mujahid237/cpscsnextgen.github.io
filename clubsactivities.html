Sports Activities

Cantonment Public School & College, Saidpur

To enhance physical fitness, develop motor skills, improve mental capabilities, and foster social and personal development the sports club of cantonment Public School & College, Saidpur has been formed. The other objectives include improving athletic performance, building teamwork, promoting sportsmanship, and increasing awareness of health and wellness. Our institution runs different sports activities for the overall development of the students. The main aims and objectives of the sports activities of our institution are physical and motor Skill Development, mental and cognitive benefits, social and personal development. Sports activities will also give them :

Health Education: Sports activities can raise awareness about healthy lifestyles, proper nutrition, and the importance of physical activity for overall well-being.

Recreation and Leisure: Sports provide opportunities for recreation, fun, and social interaction, contributing to a positive and enjoyable lifestyle.

Cultural Understanding: Participating in different sports can expose individuals to various cultures and traditions, promoting diversity and understanding.

Gender Equality: Sports can help break down gender stereotypes and empower girls and women, promoting equality and inclusivity.

At present a variety of events are arranged in our Institution around the year. These are:

  • Annual Sports.
  • Inter House Cricket Tournament.
  • Inter House Football Tournament.
  • Inter House Handball Tournament.
  • Inter House Badminton Tournament.
  • Inter House Chess Tournament.
  • Inter House Volleyball Tournament.
  • Inter House Basket Ball Tournament.
  • Inter House Table Tennis Tournament.
  • Inter House Scrabble Tournament
  • Sports Club.

BNCC Activities

BANGLADESH NATIONAL CADET CORPS (BNCC)

BANGLADESH NATIONAL CADET CORPS (BNCC)

Bangladesh National Cadet Corps (BNCC) of Cantonment Public School and College, Saidpur was established on 10 May 2004. Here one Senior Mix Platoon of BNCC which includes the students of class XI and XII. This platoon has 31 BNCC cadets both male and female. Appointed teachers are 2 Lt. Dr. Md. Ashraful Kabir (CCO-0377), Senior Lecturer (Zoology); Acting PUO Ferdousi Yesmin, Lecturer (Bangla); Acting PUO Md. Lokman Hossain, Lecturer (Chemistry) have been assigned with the duty of BNCC. Cadets are trained by a trainer (staff) from Bangladesh Army twice a week.

In order to ensure knowledge, discipline, and unity of the students, this unit has been formed.

BNCC Cadets take part in the following activities―

  • Capsule training (yearly 2);
  • Annual training camp (yearly 1);
  • Central training camp (yearly 1);
  • Tree plantation program;
  • Natural disaster program;
  • Preventing eve-teasing;
  • Anti-drug campaign; and
  • Various social and cultural activities

Objectives:

  • To develop individual character, leadership qualities, and sense of dedication to national affairs;
  • To provide military training to young students so as to stimulate their interest for the defense of the country;
  • To provide disciplined volunteers for the national development programs and during natural disasters;
  • To provide a second-line defense in the face of external aggression and invasion;
  • To build up a reserve of potential leaders for various walks of national life including the armed forces.

Motto of BNCC: Grooming Future Leader

The other name of BNCC is discipline. The disciplined activities BNCC cadets are exhibited when our institution authority engage them in various works. They have shown their great caliber during the July uprising by maintaining the discipline of Traffic. Because of their extra-ordinary talent many of them have got admission as Officer Cadets in Bangladesh army.

Scouts Activities

Cantonment Public School and College, Saidpur

Scouts Activities

To foster the development of young people physically, intellectually, socially, and spiritually Scouts has always played an important role. This is achieved through a variety of methods, including character development, citizenship training, personal fitness, and leadership development. Scouting also aims to provide opportunities for skill acquisition, belonging to a group, and contributing to a worthy cause. To build up ideal and responsible citizens, Scouts has always played a pioneering role. Scouts unit of Cantonment Public School and College, Saidpur was established on 1st May, 2018. Here a mixed Scouts unit of Girl and a Boy has been running well with its activities. It includes the students of class Six to Ten. This unit has 32 girl scouts and 32 boy scouts in number. A senior teacher of high school section, Rowshon Ara Chowdhury has been assigned with the duty of unit leader. This Scouts unit has been working for the physical, mental and moral development of the students and encourages them to participate in various social service activities.

Functions: Scouts are trained by Scouts unit leader twice a week. Scouts take part in the following activities:

  • Yearly Training Camp
  • B.P. Day
  • Tree plantation Programme
  • Electricity Saving Campaign
  • Preventing Eve-teasing
  • Anti-drug Campaign
  • Various Cleaning Activities
  • Helping Flood-affected people
  • Various Special Activities

Objectives:

  • To develop individual character, leadership qualities and sense of dedication to national affairs
  • To provide disciplined volunteers for the national development programmes and during natural disasters.
  • To build up a reserve of potential leaders for various walks of national life
  • To develop potential human resources for the development of nation

The display of the organized activities of the Scouts is very much visible when they are given responsibility to maintain the discipline of the institution during the time some kind of both institutional and national programmes are held. They have been awarded by the Local Government Authority for their outstanding performances in different camps.
