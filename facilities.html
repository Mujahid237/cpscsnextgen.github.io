Campus Facilities

ক্যাম্পাস সুবিধাসমূহ

শিক্ষার্থীদের শারীরিক, মানসিক এবং অ্যাকাডেমিক উন্নয়নের জন্য আমাদের ক্যাম্পাসে রয়েছে বিশ্বমানের সুযোগ-সুবিধা।

01

Hostel

ছাত্রবাস ও ছাত্রীনিবাস

দূর-দূরান্তের শিক্ষার্থীদের জন্য নিরিবিলি, নিরাপদ এবং সুশৃঙ্খল আবাসন ব্যবস্থা। রয়েছে আধুনিক মেস সুবিধা ও সার্বক্ষণিক নিরাপত্তা।

02

Library

কেন্দ্রীয় গ্রন্থাগার ও ই-লাইব্রেরি

হাজারো দেশি-বিদেশি বই, গবেষণা সাময়িকী এবং ডিজিটাল ই-বুকের সমাহারে সমৃদ্ধ একটি আধুনিক ও নিরিবিলি লাইব্রেরি।

03

Laboratories

বিজ্ঞান ও কম্পিউটার ল্যাব

পদার্থ, রসায়ন, জীববিজ্ঞান এবং আধুনিক হাই-স্পিড ইন্টারনেট সমৃদ্ধ কম্পিউটার ল্যাবের বাস্তব ব্যবহারিক সুবিধা।

04

Health Center

স্বাস্থ্যসেবা কেন্দ্র

শিক্ষার্থীদের প্রাথমিক চিকিৎসা ও জরুরি শারীরিক অসুস্থতায় দ্রুত সেবা প্রদানের জন্য দক্ষ মেডিকেল টিম ও ফার্স্ট এইড ব্যবস্থা।

05

Counselling Center

কাউন্সেলিং ও ক্যারিয়ার সেন্টার

শিক্ষার্থীদের মানসিক চাপ নিরসন, দিকনির্দেশনা প্রদান এবং ভবিষ্যতের ক্যারিয়ার গঠনে অভিজ্ঞ কাউন্সেলরদের পরামর্শ সেবা।

Boys’ Hostel

Cantonment Public School & College, Saidpur

To serve the purpose of accommodating the remote students a modern hostel has been functioning since the 1st January, 2015. The hostel has a 5th floor foundation, but currently it is a three storied building with the accommodation capacity of around 250 students. The hostel provides a very pleasant environment for the students to have their proper meal study, accommodation, health service, recreation, safety and security. The Hostel is directly conducted by a Hostel Super, an Assistant Hostel Super, an Assistant Teacher (Residential), a hostel manager and fifteen supporting employees. It is also being observed and monitored accordingly by different subject teachers every day. Above all, the Principal of the institution has direct supervision for the overall betterment of the students.

Facilities

Safety and Security:

  • 24/7 Security guard
  • CCTV surveillance
  • Biometric or Card access entry.

Accommodation:

  • Spacious room furnished with Bed, Mattress, Pillow, Study Table-Chair, Fans and others.
  • Hygienic washrooms with sufficient toiletries.
  • AC Room for Parents’ waiting.
  • A Guest Room.

Food:

  • Everyday meals with proper nutrition.
  • Meal schedule (breakfast, lunch, snacks and dinner & improved diet twice a month)
  • Filtered drinking water.
  • Meals served in a neat and clean dining hall.

Cleanliness and Maintenance:

  • Daily cleaning of washrooms
  • Regular sanitization of common areas
  • Waste disposal system

Health Care:

  • First Aid kit
  • 24/7 Emergency Medical service by an MBBS doctor.
  • Emergency treatment provided by CMH.

Recreation and Common Areas:

  • A huge Recreation Room.
  • A Large TV
  • Indoor games (Carom, Chess, Table Tennis etc.)
  • Outdoor games (Badminton, Volleyball, Football, Cricket etc.)
  • Daily newspaper.
  • Computer with internet facilities.

Library

Cantonment Public School And College, Saidpur

There is an enriched library in Cantonment Public School And College, Saidpur. It has a considerable collection of books. Around five thousand books from different walks of life like history books, biography, religious books, liberation war books, science fictions, novels, dramas, short stories, magazines, journals, Olympiad books, different reference books are kept here in an arranged way. The library is run by a librarian along with three supporting members. The students, teachers & employees can borrow book by using their ID through online system. It plays a significant role in the field of teaching-learning activities of this institution.

Activities:-

  • All of the students, teachers and employees can borrow books according to their demand from library .
  • During leisure time they can study in library and take part in co-curriculum activities.
  • Students has regular scheduled classes in the library.
  • Everybody can read daily newspaper and magazine in library .
  • Students can practise by themselves for GK, Olympiad, Drawing, debate, spelling etc. for different kinds of competitions both nationally and internationally.

Library facilities:

  • i. Students and teachers can borrow books
  • ii. Students have regular scheduled class periods in the library

Health Centre

Medical Centre of CPSCS

Considering the obvious importance of attention to the health of the students, teachers and staffs medical centre was established in 2010 inside the campus. CPSCSauthority appointed a medical officer (MBBS) and a medical assistantto serve the purpose.

Now a well established medical centre provides the students with all possible modern techniques and equipments. Authority provides medications and health and hygiene facilities. Now, the centre has an oxygen cylinder, a nebulizer machine, a refrigerator, surgical instruments, diabetic kit and so on.

All kinds of possible facilities are available here. The physical and mental health of the students are the main concern. The services also include counseling, motivation, awareness about personal hygiene, medical tips, emergency ambulance services etc.If also provides 24 hour emergency health services to the students residing at hostels.

The medical centre aims to promote the wellbeing of every individuals related to CPSCS and encourage healthy lifestyles, prevent disease, illness and injury.
